# Held-out human data

Human generalisation rests on **one genuinely independent study**. Everything else human is
either the same study with a tissue withheld, or a proteogenomics cell line. That is worth
stating plainly: if a reviewer discounts Ruiz-Orera, there is no second human study behind it.

```{list-table}
:header-rows: 1
:widths: 26 30 44

* - Dataset
  - Role
  - What it does and does not test
* - **Hepatocytes**
  - held-out tissue (LOTO)
  - Same Chothani panel, same protocol, same lab, one tissue withheld from training. Tests cross-TISSUE generalisation only.
* - **Ruiz-Orera**
  - held-out study
  - iPSC-cardiomyocyte, different lab, different cell type. The only true cross-STUDY human test.
* - **B721.221**
  - ground truth
  - Deepest Ribo-seq in the project (18.8 M P-sites). Used as the measured-translation reference, not as a training holdout. See {doc}`/benchmarks`.
* - **HBL-1**
  - proteogenomics
  - Weakest library here (2 libs, 128 K P-sites, 34 nt mode, lowest f0). Weight its result below the others.
```

## Reading it

- **Annotated ORFs transfer essentially intact** across tissue and study.
- **dORF and internal are at the floor everywhere.** They are the hardest classes for the caller,
  not just for the model.
- **uORF is the most dataset-dependent class**, and that spread is the largest unexplained gap in
  the table.
